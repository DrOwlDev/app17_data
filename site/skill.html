Polymarket edge: how early is the forecast good?

Markets settle on resolution-source daily min (Low) or daily max (High). Hourly forecasts are paired within ±30 minutes. Hit = |forecast − observed| ≤ 0.4°C. L / Lmin / Lmax = hours-before where forecasts stay good enough (hit ≥ 80% or MAE ≤ 0.4°C) — smaller is better for earlier Polymarket entries. Also: 14-day extrema timing (min before 6am vs after 6pm) and lock-hour histograms. Tip: HKO settlement Absolute can differ from the live hkoc curve — don’t treat chart as settlement for HK.
