How good is the forecast source?

Forecasts are paired to the nearest observation within ±30 minutes of each valid local hour. Hit means |forecast − observed| ≤ 0.5°C. Time-to-skill L is how many hours before the event the forecast becomes good enough (hit rate ≥ 80% or MAE ≤ 0.5°C for leads ≤ L).
